# If something goes wrong

Copy the one that fits, fill in the brackets, and send it to your Zo assistant in the same chat where you built your site.

## It did not publish, or the link does not open

My website did not publish, or the link does not open. Find out what went wrong, fix it, and publish again. Keep the page the same. When it is live, send me the working link.

## It looks plain, with no fonts or colours

My page looks plain, with no fonts or colours. Make sure it loads only these two stylesheets, exactly as written, and no styling of your own:
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/chintheman/workshop-design-system@main/tokens.css
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/chintheman/workshop-design-system@main/base.css
Then publish again and send me the link.

## A detail is wrong

On my website, change [what is wrong] to [the right text]. Change nothing else. Publish again and send me the link.

## I want to remove something

Remove [what to remove] from my website. Change nothing else. Publish again and send me the link.

## I want to take it down

Take my website offline. Tell me when it is no longer live.
