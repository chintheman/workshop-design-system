# Before the workshop (about 15 minutes)

1. Install Telegram on your phone and sign in.
2. Make a Zo account at zo.computer, using the email you check most.
3. Choose the Basic plan (US$18 a month, about S$23).
4. Open Zo once and send your assistant "Hello". If it replies, you're ready.
5. Bring your laptop, charged, plus your business details: what you do, who you serve, up to 3 services, and the contact details you're happy to share publicly.
